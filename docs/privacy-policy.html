Privacy Policy for Pitch Trainer

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Pitch Trainer ("the app") is developed by Adhham Zaatri ("we", "us"). This page explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Summary

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no internet access permission, contains no analytics or advertising software development kits (SDKs), and does not communicate with any server.

What the app stores

The app saves your preferences (such as selected instrument range and difficulty settings) and your practice statistics locally on your device only, using Android's standard local storage mechanism. This data:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is not accessible to us or any third party,
  • is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Permissions

The app requests the MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS permission, which is used only to manage audio playback behavior on your device (for example, routing the training tone correctly). This permission does not grant access to your microphone, contacts, files, or any personal information. The app does not request microphone access and does not record audio.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. No data is shared with third parties because no data leaves the device in the first place.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that requires data collection — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact
